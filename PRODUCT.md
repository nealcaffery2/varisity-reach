# Product

**What:** The athletics site for Hillsboro High School (Hillsboro, Illinois),
home of the Hiltoppers. Black and orange. South Central Conference, IHSA.

**Who it serves, in order:**

1. **College coaches and recruiters.** They need to find a Topper who fits a
   need (sport, class, size, grades) in under a minute, see production and
   film, and reach the right adult. Recruit board, profiles, print sheet.
2. **Athletes and families.** A profile they are proud to send, that looks
   right on a phone and in a text thread. Share bar, reels, OG images.
3. **Fans and the town.** Highlights, results, what's next. Home page, team
   pages, news.

**Surfaces and modes** (impeccable):

| Surface | Mode |
| --- | --- |
| Home | Persuade |
| Team pages, news | Read |
| Recruit board, stats | Operate |
| Athlete profile | Persuade for the athlete, Operate for the coach |
| Reels | Experience |

**Source of truth for facts:** hillsboroschools.net, hhs.hillsboroschools.net
/athletics and hiltopperathletics.org. This site links to them for anything
that changes on game day.

**Privacy floor:** athletes are minors. A real profile goes up only with a
signed media release, shows no home address, phone or personal email, and
routes contact through the athletic office. Sample profiles are marked and
kept out of search.
