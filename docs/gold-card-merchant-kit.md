# Gold card merchant kit: Hillsboro

How to sign up businesses for the Hillsboro gold card. Nothing below is
agreed. Every offer on the live card needs a signed one-page agreement from
the owner first; until then the site shows sample offers only.

## The pitch, in one paragraph

Every family in the bleachers carries the card on their phone. Your deal sits
on it all season. Each use is a scan, so you see exactly how many people came
in because of the Toppers. The card costs families $20 and the money goes to
the booster club. You pay nothing to be on it.

## Tiers

| Tier | What the business gives | What it gets |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Gold (4 to 6 spots) | A deal people talk about, tied to the team | Top of the card, logo on the scan screen, a thank-you in game-night posts |
| Silver | A strong everyday deal | Listed with the offer and a map link |
| Bronze | A simple discount | Listed |

## Offers that get used

Deals tied to the team get redeemed and shared more than flat percentages.

- Free large pizza for the whole card the week after a shutout (cap it: first 50 scans)
- Buy one get one, the Monday after a win
- Free car wash homecoming week
- Free cone for any athlete in uniform after a home game
- $1 off coffee all season, free drink the first visit
- 15% off one item a month (hardware, farm supply)
- $15 off a full service oil change

(headtohillsboro.net/eat-drink) and the Hillsboro Area Chamber of Commerce
(447 S Main St, hillsborochamber.net). Confirm each one is open before calling.

## The ask, by email or in person

> Hi, I'm with the Hillsboro booster club. We're putting the Toppers fundraiser
> card on families' phones this season, and we'd like [business] on it. You
> pick the deal. Every use is a scan, so you'll know how many people came in
> because of it. It costs you nothing, and the card money goes to the team.
> Could I stop by this week with a one-page agreement?

## After they say yes

1. They fill in the form at /gold-card/ ("Own a business?") or you do it with them.
2. The offer saves as pending. Send the one-page agreement (offer, limits,
   season dates, either side can end it with two weeks' notice).
3. When it comes back signed, staff set the offer to live. It appears on every
   card the next time a holder opens it.
4. At season's end, send each business its scan count.
